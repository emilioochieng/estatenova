How a listing gets approved

1

Account verification

Identity and, where relevant, business registration and agent credentials.

2

Authority to advertise

The advertiser must have permission to market the property.

3

Property documents

Title, ownership, lease or other supporting documents are reviewed as appropriate.

4

Listing moderation

Information, photos, pricing and misleading claims are checked.

5

Publication

The listing is approved, the review is recorded and its verification status is shown.

A verified advertiser does not guarantee every claim about a property. Always check ownership and legal interests through official processes and qualified professionals before you pay or sign anything.
